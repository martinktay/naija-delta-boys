# Five-minute Android Chrome verification

Status: `REAL_DEVICE_TOUCH: UNVERIFIED`

Use a mid-range physical Android phone in Chrome. Keep the device in portrait orientation for the first pass, then repeat boarding and steering once in landscape.

1. Load Otu Creek Waterfront and confirm the D-pad and both action buttons are visible.
2. Hold each direction for two seconds. Confirm movement continues while held and stops immediately on release.
3. Drag one held pointer from one D-pad direction to another. Confirm direction changes without a stuck input.
4. Start moving, then cancel by leaving the control area, backgrounding Chrome and returning. Confirm movement stops each time.
5. Approach the south jetty and confirm `TALK / USE` boards only within the authorised jetty range.
6. Hold boat steering for five seconds, change direction twice, then release. Confirm continuous motion, immediate stop and water-only navigation.
7. Press `TALK / USE` away from a dock and confirm disembarking is rejected.
8. Return to a marked jetty, disembark and confirm the player resumes walking on land.
9. Use `ENTER WATER`, swim, observe stamina decreasing, then allow it to expire.
10. Confirm one life and ₦250 are deducted and the player safely respawns without losing mission progress.
11. Reload Chrome and confirm mission/money persist and the reward cannot be collected twice.

Record device model, Android version, Chrome version, orientation, approximate frame rate and any stuck-input reproduction steps.
